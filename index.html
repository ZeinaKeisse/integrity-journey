Hi all,

For this assignment I created The Integrity Journey, an interactive guide that walks first-year undergraduates through seven academic integrity decisions, from reading the assignment to submitting it.

I worked with two tools. I started with an HTML website, built with the help of generative AI, because I wanted students to move through the stops, collect stamps, and see their progress. Later I experimented with Genially, which was new to me. I really enjoyed it: the clickable cards, pop-up explanations, and visual map make it very engaging, and I would happily use it again. In the end, though, the HTML version was the one that best carried my idea and the learning objectives, so it became my main artifact, with Genially as a supplementary exploration.

The integrity journey
Two weeks, one essay, seven stops

Every sentence you submit has a history.

Academic integrity means being honest about that history: which ideas are yours, which came from someone else, and which tools helped you. Over the next seven stops you'll follow one essay from the day it's assigned to the day it's submitted.

Where you're headed: a finished paragraph

Many first-year students assume university writing is about having original ideas, but most of it is a conversation with ideas other people have already had. The International Center for Academic Integrity (2021) identifies six values: “honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility, and courage” (p. 4). These values help academic communities make ethical decisions and put their principles into practice (International Center for Academic Integrity, 2021). I used an AI tool to suggest an order for these points, and I noted that in my disclosure statement.

    Stuck somewhere on the journey?

    Most integrity problems start as a time problem. Asking early is always better than a shortcut.

    I don't understand what's allowed

    Email your instructor or TA with the specific question ("Can I use AI to check grammar on this assignment?"). They'd much rather answer a question than open a case.

    I'm going to miss the deadline

    Ask for an extension as early as possible and explain briefly. A late assignment is recoverable; a misconduct finding follows you much longer.

    I'm not sure I'm citing correctly

    Book time at the writing centre or with a librarian. They can check your citations and help you set up a citation manager like Zotero.

    I've been contacted about a concern

    Respond promptly and honestly, read your institution's policy, and know you usually have the right to an advisor or support person. Your student union or ombudsperson can explain the process.
